Track your snoring. Understand your sleep.

SnoozePulse listens overnight for snoring, keeps short clips of the events that matter, and shows a morning summary. All processing and storage stay on your phone.

On this device

Detection, scoring, and playback run locally. There is no account and no cloud sync for your sleep audio.

Only what matters

The app records short snore snippets, not the whole night, so you can replay the events that stood out.

Morning summary

See duration, snore count, loudness, and a timeline you can play back after you slide to end the session.
