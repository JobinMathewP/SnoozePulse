1. Who we are

This policy describes how the SnoozePulse mobile app (“SnoozePulse”, “the app”, “we”) handles information. SnoozePulse is developed by the maintainer of the public project at github.com/JobinMathewP/SnoozePulse.

The app has no user accounts and no backend that stores your sleep data.

2. Summary

SnoozePulse uses the microphone during a sleep session to detect snoring on the device, and may save short audio snippets plus session metrics on that same device. You can start a session yourself, or opt in to automatic tracking so the app may start around your usual bedtime when the phone looks settled. That information is not uploaded to us. We do not sell data and we do not track you across apps or websites.

3. Data processed on your device

When you use SnoozePulse, the app may process on this phone:

  • Microphone audio, used only to detect snoring and to save short snore snippets you can play back.
  • Session metrics derived from that audio, such as duration, snore count, loudness, scores, and a timeline.
  • Your optional bedtime and wake times, and whether you turned on automatic sleep tracking. Automatic tracking is off until you enable it in Settings.
  • Motion from the accelerometer, charging state, and whether you are using the phone, used only to decide if the device looks settled for the night. These signals do not count steps or track workouts, and they are not used to infer that you are asleep.
  • Battery level and Low Power Mode, used to show readiness warnings, to save or skip a night when the battery is too low, and to simplify the on-screen waveform.
  • Local database records and audio files stored in the app’s private storage on your phone.

Processing happens on the device. Under Apple’s App Privacy definition, data that never leaves the device is not “collected” by us.

4. What we do not collect

  • We do not create an account or ask for your email in the app.
  • We do not upload audio, snippets, or sleep metrics to our servers.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs that send your sleep data off the device.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

5. Permissions

Microphone. Required to detect snoring. Capture starts when you tap Start Sleep Session, or — only if you have turned on automatic tracking — around your scheduled sleep window when the phone appears settled. The microphone is not left open all evening “just in case.” Capture stops when you slide to end the session, when the wake window completes, or when the session is otherwise closed. The system may show a recording indicator while the microphone is in use, including while the screen is locked.

Motion. On some devices the app reads the accelerometer at a low rate to tell whether the phone is still. This is optional for automatic tracking. Denying motion does not block a session you start yourself.

Notifications. Local notices only — nothing is sent from a SnoozePulse server. Android shows an ongoing foreground-service notice while a session is running. After a saved night, the app may show a morning summary notification on iOS and Android. Tapping it opens that night’s summary on this device.

You can revoke permissions in system Settings. The app cannot monitor sleep without the microphone. You can turn automatic tracking off at any time in SnoozePulse Settings.

6. Storage and deletion

Session history and snippets stay on your device until you delete them in the app or remove the app. Deleting SnoozePulse removes the local database and stored snippets.

If you back up your phone with Apple iCloud, Google Backup, or another device backup tool, those backups are controlled by that provider’s policy, not by SnoozePulse.

7. Children

SnoozePulse is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Not medical advice

SnoozePulse is a wellness product. It does not diagnose, treat, or prevent sleep apnea or any other medical condition. Scores and counts are informational only. Talk to a qualified clinician about health concerns.

9. This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting them may cause GitHub to process technical data such as IP address, as described in GitHub’s privacy statement. That is separate from the app. The app does not send your sleep data to GitHub or to us.

10. Your rights

Because sleep data stays on your phone, you already have access to it in the app. You can delete it by removing sessions or uninstalling SnoozePulse. If you are in a region with additional privacy rights and believe we hold personal information about you, contact us using the details below.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue on the public repository: github.com/JobinMathewP/SnoozePulse/issues.
